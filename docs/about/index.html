About · 35+ years

Quality materials. Trusted service.

For more than 35 years, Uresco has been a trusted source for quality building materials throughout the Pacific Northwest and all of Alaska.

01Who we are

Independent, and built for distance.

As one of the largest independent building-material suppliers serving Alaska and the Pacific Northwest, Uresco offers institutional, commercial and residential builders the best selection, pricing and service.

Our reliable service includes dependable delivery to job sites down the road or across an ocean. From our headquarters in Kent and our distribution centers in Anchorage and Fairbanks, we supply everything from framing lumber and engineered beams to siding, insulation, windows, cabinets and the hardware that holds it all together.

Lumber by UCM

02Our mission

Uresco provides a trusted and dependable source of quality construction materials, a knowledgeable and resourceful sales staff, and we are committed to professional and prompt service.

That commitment lets our customers buy their materials with confidence that they will receive the best products at the best prices.
