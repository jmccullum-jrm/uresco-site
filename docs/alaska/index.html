01Reach

Built for places without a lumber yard.

Two Alaska yards on the road system, a Kent yard on Puget Sound, and the freight lanes that connect them to the rest of the state.

02How a remote package works

One call. Every leg.

Remote jobs fail on the details: a missing box of hangers, a load that misses the last barge. Our process is built around not letting that happen.

  1. Plans in, take-off outSend drawings or a list. We build the take-off and price it delivered.
  2. Package and crateWe assemble the complete package and crate, band and wrap it for the trip.
  3. Book the freightWe coordinate the carrier — truck, ocean freight, barge or air — against your schedule and the season.
  4. Dock or site deliveryDelivered to the dock or straight to the site, with one point of contact the whole way.

03Ways we move freight

Road, sea or air.

The road system

Job sites on the road system — Southcentral, the Interior and the highways north — are reached by truck from our Anchorage and Fairbanks yards.

Ocean freight & barge

Southeast, coastal and western Alaska are reached by scheduled ocean carriers and seasonal barge service. We coordinate bookings and get your package to the dock.

Air cargo

Some communities are reached only by air. Tell us where you’re building and we’ll work out how to get it there.

Plan around the season. Barge service to western and northern Alaska runs only in the ice-free months, and the last sailings fill up. Call early and we’ll plan back from the sailing you need.

05Questions

Remote delivery, answered.

Can you deliver to my community?

Delivery is available anywhere in Alaska, from Sitka to Utqiaġvik. Tell us where the job is and we will work out the route — road, ocean freight, barge or air — and price it.

When should I order for barge season?

Barge service to western and northern Alaska runs only in the ice-free months, and the last sailings fill up. Call early in the year: we plan backward from the sailing you need so the package is built, crated and at the dock in time.

Do you ship complete building packages?

Yes. We are fully equipped to handle remote-location and export building packages — framing, envelope, openings and hardware — so the whole job ships together.

Can you work from my plans?

Send drawings or a materials list. We will build the take-off, check it for gaps and quote it. Design services are available for projects with unique needs.

Who coordinates the shipping?

We work with you on all aspects of shipping coordination, dock delivery and rural Alaska delivery, so you have one point of contact from quote to landing.
