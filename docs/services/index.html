Services

The service is the product.

A knowledgeable and resourceful sales staff, committed to professional and prompt service — from the first take-off to the last delivery.

01 · Estimates

Estimates & material take-offs

Send drawings and we build the take-off: the materials list for the job, priced and ready to order. On a remote job it catches the gaps before they become a second freight bill.

  • Send drawings, a list or a sketch
  • Material take-offs and estimates
  • Quoted by the yard nearest your job

02 · Design

Design services

For projects that need more than a list, our team offers design services for your unique project needs, along with take-off assistance.

  • Design help for unique project needs
  • Take-off assistance
  • Engineered floor, beam and truss packages

03 · Job-site

Job-site delivery

Our boom trucks and truck-mounted Moffett forklifts set materials where the crew needs them, not at the curb.

  • Boom trucks
  • Truck-mounted Moffett forklifts
  • Materials set where the crew needs them

04 · Remote

Remote & export packages

We are fully equipped to handle remote-location and export building packages, so the whole job ships together and nothing gets left on the dock.

  • Complete building packages
  • Remote sites anywhere in Alaska
  • Export packages

05 · Shipping

Shipping coordination

We work with you on every part of getting materials there: carrier bookings, dock delivery and the last leg into rural Alaska.

  • Carrier coordination
  • Dock delivery
  • Rural Alaska delivery

06 · Crating

Crating & packaging

Long hauls and multiple handoffs are hard on materials. We crate, band and wrap loads so they arrive the way they left.

  • Crating
  • Packaging for long hauls
  • Export and remote-site loads
