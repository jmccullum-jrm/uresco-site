General notes

Who you are buying from.

The short version.

  1. 01

    We sell wholesale.

    Uresco is a wholesale dealer to institutional, commercial and residential builders.

    Contractor accounts
  2. 02

    We are independent.

    One of the largest independent building material suppliers serving Alaska and the Pacific Northwest.

  3. 03

    We have been at it for more than 35 years.

    A trusted source for quality building materials throughout the Pacific Northwest and all of Alaska.

  4. 04

    Three yards work as one team.

    Kent is the headquarters. Anchorage serves Southcentral Alaska and Fairbanks serves the Interior. Whichever one you call can help.

    The three yards
  5. 05

    Remote delivery is our specialty.

    We are fully equipped to handle remote-location and export building packages, and delivery is available anywhere in Alaska, from Sitka to Utqiaġvik (Barrow).

  6. 06
