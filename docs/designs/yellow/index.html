Remote & export delivery

Down the road. Across an ocean.

We specialize in getting materials to places without a lumber yard: shipping coordination, dock delivery and rural Alaska delivery, from Sitka to Utqiaġvik.

  1. Send plans or a list. We build the take-off and quote it.
  2. We package it. Sorted, crated and wrapped for the trip.
  3. We coordinate the freight. Truck, ocean freight, barge or air.
  4. It lands where you build. At the dock or on the site.
