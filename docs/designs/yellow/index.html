Remote & export delivery

Down the road. Across an ocean.

We specialize in getting materials to places without a lumber yard: shipping coordination, dock delivery and rural Alaska delivery, from Sitka to Utqiaġvik.

  1. Send plans or a list. We help with the take-off and the quote.
  2. We package it. Crated and packed for the trip.
  3. We work out the shipping with you. Typically truck, ocean freight, barge or air.
  4. Delivered where you build. To the dock or straight to the site.
