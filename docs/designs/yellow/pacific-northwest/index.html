Job-site delivery

Two ways off the truck.

To job sites around the Puget Sound region, by boom truck and Moffett forklift.

Boom truck

A crane on the truck lifts the load off the deck and sets it down on site.

Moffett forklift

A forklift that rides on the back of the truck, then carries the load across the site.
