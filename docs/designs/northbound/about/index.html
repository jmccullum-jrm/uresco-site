About Uresco · More than 35 years

Who you are buying from.

Uresco Construction Materials is a wholesale dealer in building materials, with yards in Kent, Anchorage and Fairbanks. For more than 35 years we have supplied builders across the Pacific Northwest and all of Alaska.

Request a quote Call a yard

In short

We sell building materials, wholesale, to institutional, commercial and residential builders across the Pacific Northwest and all of Alaska. Our specialty is the hard part: a complete building package, delivered to a site with no road to it.

35+

01Thirty-five years

Thirty-five rings and counting.

For more than 35 years, Uresco has been a trusted source for quality building materials throughout the Pacific Northwest and all of Alaska, and is one of the largest independent building material suppliers serving Alaska and the Pacific Northwest.

Drawn with one ring for each of those years. The last ring is still open.

    A trusted, dependable source of quality construction materials.

  2. 02

    A knowledgeable, resourceful sales staff.

  3. 03

    Professional and prompt service.

    Dependable site delivery to job sites down the road or across an ocean: boom trucks and truck-mounted Moffett forklifts around the Puget Sound region, dock and rural delivery anywhere in Alaska.
