From 47.4°N to61.2°N

Packed once, for the whole trip.

A building bound for a place with no lumber yard cannot be one box of hangers short. So a remote package is planned from the far end back.

  1. 01

    Plans in, take-off out

    Send drawings or a list. We build the take-off, check it for gaps and price it delivered.

  2. 02

    Pulled and packaged

    The complete package is assembled, then crated, banded and wrapped for the trip and every handoff on it.

  3. 03

    Freight, coordinated

    Truck, ocean freight, barge or air. We work with you on the shipping, against your schedule and the season.

  4. 04

    Dock or site

    Delivered to the dock, or straight to where you build.

One complete package beats a second freight bill.

04The last mile64.8°N → to 71.3°N

From 64.8°N to71.3°N

Past the end of the road.

Much of Alaska has no road in. Freight arrives by barge in the ice-free season and by air all year, and Southeast is reached by sea. Remote delivery is what we specialize in.

Delivery is available anywhere in Alaska, from Sitka to Utqiaġvik.

We work with you on every part of getting it there: shipping coordination, dock delivery and rural Alaska delivery, with remote-location and export building packages a specialty.
