02How a remote package moves

One call. Every leg.

Remote jobs fail on the details: a missing box of hangers, a load that misses the last barge. The work is built around not letting that happen.

  1. 01

    Plans in, take-off out

    Send drawings or a list. We build the take-off, check it for gaps and price it delivered.

  2. 02

    Package and crate

    We assemble the complete package, then crate, band and wrap it for the trip.

  3. 03

    Book the freight

    We coordinate the carrier, whether truck, ocean freight, barge or air, against your schedule and the season.

  4. 04

    Dock or site delivery

    Delivered to the dock or straight to the site, with one point of contact the whole way.

03Ways we move freight

Road, sea or air.

Much of Alaska is off the road system. Which way a package travels depends on where it is going and what month it is.

  • The road system

    Job sites on the road system, in Southcentral, the Interior and up the highways north, are reached by truck from our Anchorage and Fairbanks yards.

  • Ocean freight & barge

    Southeast, coastal and western Alaska are reached by ocean carriers and seasonal barge service. We coordinate the bookings and get your package to the dock.

  • Air cargo

    Some communities are reached only by air. Tell us where you are building and we will work out how to get it there.

Plan around the season. Barge service to western and northern Alaska runs only in the ice-free months, and space on the last sailings goes first. Call early and we will plan back from the sailing you need.

05Questions

Remote delivery, answered.

Still wondering? The yards answer questions like these every day.

Can you deliver to my community?

Delivery is available anywhere in Alaska, from Sitka to Utqiaġvik (Barrow). Tell us where the job is and we will work out the route, by road, ocean freight, barge or air, and price it.

When should I order for barge season?

Barge service to western and northern Alaska runs only in the ice-free months, and space on the last sailings goes first. Call early in the year: we plan backward from the sailing you need, so the package is built, crated and at the dock in time.

Do you ship complete building packages?

Yes. We are fully equipped to handle remote-location and export building packages: framing, envelope, openings and hardware, so the whole job ships together.

Can you work from my plans?

Send drawings or a materials list. We will build the take-off, check it for gaps and quote it. Design services are available for projects with unique needs.

Who coordinates the shipping?

We work with you on all aspects of shipping coordination, dock delivery and rural Alaska delivery, so you have one point of contact from quote to landing.
