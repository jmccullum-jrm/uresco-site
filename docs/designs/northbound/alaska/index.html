    4. 04

      Delivered to the dock or straight to the site.

    03Ways freight moves

    Road, sea or air.

    Much of Alaska is off the road system. Which way a package travels depends on where it is going and what month it is.

    • The road system

      Job sites on the road system, in Southcentral, the Interior and up the highways north, are typically reached by truck from our Anchorage and Fairbanks yards.

    • Ocean freight & barge

      Southeast Alaska is reached by sea. Coastal and western communities are typically reached by ocean carriers and, in the ice-free season, by barge. We work with you on shipping coordination and dock delivery.

    • Air cargo

      Off the road system, air runs year-round. Tell us where you are building and we will work out how to get it there.

    Plan around the season. Barge service to western and northern Alaska generally runs only in the ice-free months. Call early and we will work out the freight with you.

    Can you deliver to my community?

    Delivery is available anywhere in Alaska, from Sitka to Utqiaġvik (Barrow). Tell us where the job is and we will work out the route with you, by road, ocean freight, barge or air, and quote the package.

    When should I order for barge season?

    Early. Where freight moves by barge in the ice-free season, the window is short. Call your yard and we will work out the freight with you.

    Which yard should I call?

    Southcentral Alaska or a remote site: Anchorage. The Interior and points north: Fairbanks. Puget Sound: Kent. Any yard can help. They work as one team.

    Do you ship complete building packages?

    Yes. We are fully equipped to handle remote-location and export building packages, crated for the trip. One complete package beats a second freight bill.

    Can you work from my plans?

    Send drawings or a materials list. We help with the take-off and give you an estimate. Design services are available for your unique project needs.

    Who coordinates the shipping?

    We work with you on all aspects of shipping coordination, dock delivery and rural Alaska delivery.
