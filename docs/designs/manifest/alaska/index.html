    01

    Delivery area

    Built for places without a lumber yard.

    Three yards and the lanes that connect them to the rest of the state. Your community does not need to be on the map: tell the Anchorage or Fairbanks yard where you are building.

    02

    Ways in

    Lanes A–D

    Road, water or air.

    Much of Alaska is off the road system. How a package travels depends on where it is going and what time of year it is.

    LaneB

    By road

    Southcentral, the Interior and the highway north

    Job-site delivery by truck from our Anchorage and Fairbanks yards, with Moffett fork unload and boom trucks.

    LaneA · C

    By water

    Southeast, the coast and the western rivers

    Ocean freight generally reaches Southeast and the coast. Barges reach western Alaska and its river communities in the ice-free season. We work with you on the shipping and the dock delivery.

    LaneD

    By air

    Communities off the road system

    Some places are reached only by air. Tell us where you are building and we will work out how to get it there.

    Note 1 · Season

    Plan for the barge window. Communities off the road system generally get their freight by barge in the ice-free season and by air year-round. One complete package on the right sailing beats a second freight bill, so call early.

    03

    How a remote package works

    One list. Every leg.

    Remote jobs go wrong on the details: a missing box of hangers, a load that is not on the dock when the barge is. The whole process is built around the list being right before anything moves.

    1. 01

      Plans in, take-off out

      Send drawings or a materials list. We build the take-off and quote it, and work with you on the freight.

    2. 02

      Package and crate

      We assemble the complete package, then crate, band and wrap it for the trip and every handoff on the way.

    3. 03

      Coordinate the freight

      Truck, ocean freight, barge or air: we work with you on the shipping, against your schedule and the season.

    4. 04

      Dock or site delivery

      It lands at the dock or on the job site, anywhere in Alaska.

    Note 2 · Handoffs

    Crated for the trip, not just the truck.

    Long hauls and repeated handoffs are hard on materials. We crate, band and wrap loads so they arrive the way they left.

    • Units banded and wrapped
    • Crating and packaging
    • Dock delivery or straight to the site

    06

    Questions

    FAQ

    Remote delivery, answered.

    Five questions that come up on remote jobs.

    Ask a yard
    Q1Can you deliver to my community?

    Answer

    Yes. Delivery is available anywhere in Alaska, from Sitka to Utqiaġvik (Barrow). Tell us where the job is. We will quote the package and work out the freight with you: road, ocean freight, barge or air.

    Q2When should I order for barge season?

    Answer

    Early. Off the road system, barges generally run only in the ice-free season, so a package has to be built, crated and at the dock before the sailing it needs. Call early and we will work out the timing with you.

    Q3Do you ship complete building packages?

    Answer

    Yes. We are fully equipped to handle remote-location and export building packages: framing, envelope, openings and hardware, so the whole job ships together.

    Q4Can you work from my plans?

    Answer

    Send drawings or a materials list. We will build the take-off and quote it. Design services are available for projects with unique needs.

    Q5Who coordinates the shipping?

    Answer

    We work with you on all aspects of shipping coordination, dock delivery and rural Alaska delivery.
