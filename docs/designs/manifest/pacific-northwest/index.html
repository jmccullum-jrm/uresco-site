01

Job-site delivery

Two ways off the truck.

A load is only delivered when it is where the crew can use it. From Kent, that means one of these two machines.

Up and over.

A boom truck lifts the unit off the bed and reaches it over the curb and onto the deck. It suits a load that has to go up, or over something.

Across the site.

The forklift rides in on the back of the truck, drives off at the job and carries the unit over the ground to where the crew is working. It suits a load that has to travel.

Not sure which one your site needs? Tell the Kent yard what is there when you order: (253) 395-1211.
