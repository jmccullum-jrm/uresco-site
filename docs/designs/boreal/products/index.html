The nine product lines

Nine product lines

Try

No.LineIn the line

  1. 01

    Framing Lumber & Timbers

    Dimensional lumber and timbers for framing, including pressure-treated and fire-retardant-treated.

    • Dimensional lumberStuds, joists and rafters for wall, floor and roof framing.
    • TimbersHeavy sawn sections for posts, beams and exposed structure.
    • Pressure-treated lumberTreated lumber for decks, sills and wet exposures. Ask your yard about treatment.
    • Fire-retardant-treated lumberFor assemblies where code calls for FRT framing or sheathing.
    Quote this line: Framing Lumber & Timbers
  2. 02

    Plywood, OSB & Sheathing

    Structural panels for floors, walls and roofs, plus exterior gypsum sheathing.

    • Georgia-Pacific
    • PlywoodPanels for floors, walls and roofs. Ask your yard about grades.
    • Oriented strand board (OSB)Sheathing and subfloor panels.
    • DensGlass® Gold sheathingFiberglass-mat gypsum exterior sheathing by Georgia-Pacific.
    Quote this line: Plywood, OSB & Sheathing
  3. 03

    Engineered Wood & Trusses

    Long spans, straight members and complete floor systems.

    • Parallam®
    • I-joist floor systemsLight, straight joists for long, quiet floor spans.
    • Glulam beams (GLB)Glued-laminated beams for headers, ridges and exposed spans.
    • Laminated veneer lumber (LVL)High-strength headers, beams and rim board.
    • Parallam® PSL beamsParallel strand lumber for heavy loads and columns.
    • Truss packagesRoof and floor trusses. Send your plans.
    Quote this line: Engineered Wood & Trusses
  4. 04

    Concrete Forming Panels

    Overlaid plywood built to come off the pour clean and go back up again.

    • MDO plywoodMedium-density overlay for forming, signs and paint-grade work.
    • HDO plywoodHigh-density overlay for smooth finishes and many reuses.
    Quote this line: Concrete Forming Panels
  5. 05

    Cedar, Decking & Fencing

    Cedar and composite for siding, trim, decks and fences.

    • Cedar siding & trimCedar siding and trim. Ask your yard about profiles.
    • Cedar deckingNaturally durable decking boards.
    • Cedar fencingPickets, rails and posts.
    • Composite deckingLow-maintenance boards for decks and stairs.
    Quote this line: Cedar, Decking & Fencing
  6. 06

    Siding, Roofing & Wraps

    Fiber-cement siding, roofing and the weather barrier behind it.

    • James Hardie
    • CertainTeed
    • James Hardie® fiber-cement sidingLap, panel and trim products.
    • CertainTeed sidingSiding and trim systems.
    • RoofingRoofing materials and underlayment.
    • House wrapsWeather-resistive barriers for walls.
    Quote this line: Siding, Roofing & Wraps
  7. 07

    Insulation & Drywall

    Insulation for the envelope and drywall for the interior.

    • Rigid foam insulation (XPS & EPS)Continuous insulation for foundations, slabs, walls and roofs.
    • Fiberglass batt insulationCavity insulation for walls, floors and ceilings.
    • DrywallGypsum board for walls and ceilings.
    Quote this line: Insulation & Drywall
  8. 08

    Windows, Doors & Cabinets

    Openings and casework, packaged with the rest of the job.

    • WindowsWindows for the job. Ask your yard about makes and sizes.
    • DoorsExterior and interior doors.
    • Custom cabinetsCabinetry built to your layout.
    Quote this line: Windows, Doors & Cabinets
  9. 09

    Hardware, Fasteners & Job-Site Supplies

    The connectors, fasteners and consumables that finish a load.

    • Simpson Strong-Tie
    • Simpson Strong-Tie® hardwareHangers, ties, holdowns and structural connectors.
    • FastenersNails, screws and anchors.
    • Tarps & poly sheetingWeather protection for materials and work.
    • Caulking & adhesivesSealants and construction adhesives.
    Quote this line: Hardware, Fasteners & Job-Site Supplies
