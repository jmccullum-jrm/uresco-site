What we carry in engineered wood.

I-joist floor systems, glulam, LVL and PSL beams, and truss packages, quoted from your plans so the members match the design.

Line 03Five products

  1. 01

    I-joist floor systems

    Light, straight joists for long, quiet floor spans.

  2. 02

    Glulam beams (GLB)

    Glued-laminated beams for headers, ridges and exposed spans.

  3. 03

    Laminated veneer lumber (LVL)

    High-strength headers, beams and rim board.

  4. 04

    Parallam® PSL beams

    Parallel strand lumber for heavy loads and columns.

  5. 05

    Truss packages

    Roof and floor trusses engineered to your plans.
