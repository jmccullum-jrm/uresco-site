About Uresco · 35+ years

More than 35 years of getting it there.

The mission

Our mission, in three parts.

Here is each part, and what it means on your job.

  1. 01

    A trusted, dependable source of quality construction materials.

    Nine product lines, framing lumber to roofing, including James Hardie, CertainTeed, Simpson Strong-Tie, Georgia-Pacific DensGlass and Parallam.

    See the product lines
  2. 02

    A knowledgeable, resourceful sales staff.

    Send plans and they will help with the take-off and the estimate. Design services are available for your unique project needs.

    Take-offs and design
  3. 03

    Professional, prompt service.

    Job-site delivery by boom truck and truck-mounted Moffett forklift. Crating and packaging, shipping coordination, dock delivery and rural Alaska delivery when the job is off the road.

    All six services

All of it so you can buy with confidence that you are getting the best products at the best prices.
