Job-site delivery

Off the truck and onto the site.

Kent delivers to job sites with Moffett fork unload and boom trucks. The site decides which one you want.

Boom truck

A crane on the truck lifts bundles off the bed and sets them in place.

Truck-mounted Moffett forklift

The forklift rides in on the back of the truck, comes off at the site and carries the load across it.

Not sure which your site needs? Tell Kent what the site is like and where the load has to end up.
