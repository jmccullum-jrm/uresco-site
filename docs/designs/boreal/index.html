    From plans to site

    How a building package travels.

    The same five steps whether the site is across town or across the Gulf of Alaska.

    Building package01 / 05
    1. Plans
    2. Take-off
    3. Package
    4. Freight
    5. Delivered
    1. 01

      Send plans or a list

      Drawings, a materials list or a rough scope. For projects that need more than a list, design services are available.

      Design services
    2. 02

      We help with the take-off

      We help with the take-off and the estimate. On a remote job, a complete list is what keeps a missing box of hangers from becoming a second freight bill.

      Estimates & take-offs
    3. 03

      We package and crate it

      The complete package comes together, with crating and packaging for the trip.

      Crating & packaging
    4. 04

      We coordinate the freight

      Truck, ocean freight, barge or air. We work with you on all aspects of shipping coordination, and we plan around the season.

      Shipping coordination
    5. 05

      It lands where you build

      Dock delivery for a village off the road system, or a boom truck and Moffett forklift setting the load on a Puget Sound job site.

      Job-site delivery
